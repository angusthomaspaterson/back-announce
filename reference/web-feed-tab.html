Sources
Gathered once a day by a scheduled task: SoundCloud, Mixcloud and podcast feeds, Substack and magazine feeds, the NTS API, Rinse show pages, and the Bandcamp Daily and Radio pages. Pictures are copied in at thumbnail size. New cards stay marked until you press Mark all seen. Tap the heart on a card to save it; Saved keeps a copy, so it stays after the episode drops out of the feed. Paused sources keep being gathered in the background, so switching one back on brings its recent episodes straight in. Your paused list is saved with the feed, so it matches on phone and desktop. To add a source, tell Claude the show or account.
